# Documentation

Add real-time voice and video to your app. You join people into a **room**, they **publish** their microphone or camera, and everyone else in the room receives it. Our servers do the routing.

> **Preview.** The SDK works today against our media servers, and the API described here is what you build on. Names may still change before general availability. Join tokens and the console arrive with the beta.

## How a call works

1. Your server decides a user may join a room and gives the app a token (from the beta onward).
2. The app calls `join()`. The SDK connects to the nearest healthy server.
3. Speakers call `publish()` with a microphone or camera track. Their audio goes up once.
4. Everyone else in the room gets a `trackAdded` event and hears it. The server makes the copies.
5. Minutes are counted per person from join to leave, and billed per 1,000.
